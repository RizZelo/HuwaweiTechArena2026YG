## Stress tests of the 0.47 model (2026-11-11, `src/check_flaws.py`, no labels used)

- **Unseen room.** Removing a room's fingerprint from the model's bank and re-running that room's windows: only 51% of answers stay the same. The room is then treated as another room (d1 -> u1 100%, j1 -> h1 100%, u1 -> d1 73%, h1 -> j1 72%) or as "global" (v1 94%, i1 70%). Empty share goes from 20% to 79% (d1), 59% (j1), 1% (u1). Fingerprint distances cannot separate "own room" (median 0.058) from "nearest other room" (median 0.087): a gate that rejects every unseen room (0.05) also rejects 63% of known-room windows.
- **20% empty quota per room.** With global statistics only, d1 is called 74% empty and j1 51%, and recordings flip less between empty/occupied (19 mixed recordings vs 38 with the per-room quota). So the unlabelled rooms are probably not 20% empty each, and the per-room thresholds of d1 / j1 are wrong.
- **Bandwidth noise.** Averaging 5 or 11 short spectra (Welch) does not make the Doppler bandwidth steadier inside a recording (0.23 -> 0.24 / 0.26). Dropped.
